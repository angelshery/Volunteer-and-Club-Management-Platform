About Volunteer Hub

Connecting students with clubs, volunteer opportunities, and meaningful experiences.

Our Mission

Volunteer Hub is a centralized platform designed to help students discover campus organizations and volunteer opportunities that match their interests and skills.

Our goal is to make student involvement easier by bringing clubs, events, registrations, and volunteer-hour tracking into one place.

For Students

  • Create and manage a student profile
  • Select interests, skills, and availability
  • Discover and join student clubs
  • Search and register for volunteer events
  • Receive personalized recommendations
  • Track participation and volunteer hours

For Club Administrators

  • Create and manage student clubs
  • Publish volunteer opportunities
  • Manage student event registrations
  • Record attendance
  • Verify volunteer hours
  • View participation statistics

How It Works

  1. Create an account and log in.
  2. Complete your profile and preferences.
  3. Explore clubs and volunteer opportunities.
  4. Join clubs and register for events.
  5. Participate and track your involvement.
